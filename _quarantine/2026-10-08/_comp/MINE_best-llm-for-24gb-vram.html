How we pick

A model qualifies when its measured GGUF file plus KV cache plus runtime overhead stays under 24GB x 0.92. We choose the largest such quant (bigger quant = less degradation), then rank models by parameter count — at 24GB VRAM the best model you can run is almost always the largest one that still fits.

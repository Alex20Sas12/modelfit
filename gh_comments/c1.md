The math confirms this is a hardware limit, not a config issue. Measured GGUF sizes for Qwen3.8-Flash-Next (unsloth repo, split shards summed, pulled from the HF API): even the most aggressive quant (UD-IQ1_S) is a 72.5 GB file, and the ~9.9 GiB dense portion alone nearly fills the 11.50 GiB free on a 12 GB 3060 before any cache is allocated. With the fixed two-layer prefill buffer (~3 GiB), you're ~1.9 GiB over budget even in the default config — which matches the negative budget in your log (needs 3046637568 B > budget -1868787708 B).

`--moe-backend cpu` offloads expert compute, but the dense weights + prefill buffer must still be GPU-resident: realistically this model needs a 16 GB card (4060 Ti 16GB / 4080) as a floor, not 12 GB.

For reference, measured requirements per quant for this model (refreshed daily from the HF API): https://modelfit-eight.vercel.app/unsloth-qwen3-8-flash-next-gguf/

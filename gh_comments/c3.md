One correction to the framing: the failure at 131K context is not DeepSeek's KV cache being large — it's MLA being *small* and the allocator overshooting. DeepSeek-Coder-V2-Lite uses MLA (kv_lora_rank 512 + qk_rope_head_dim 64, 27 layers), so the entire 131K-token KV cache is only ~4.1 GB: 14.07 GB weights + 4.1 GB KV + overhead ≈ 19.3 GB — it genuinely fits a 24 GB A10G at full 128K context.

The 11.5–34.5 GB context-buffer allocations in the log are 3–8x the true MLA KV size. That points at the doubling logic in `PrepareContext` (src/score_cmd.cc) sizing the buffer with a generic GQA/MHA formula (2 × layers × kv_heads × head_dim × ctx) instead of the MLA compressed dimension. This model reports num_key_value_heads=16 / head_dim=128, so the naive formula gives 2 × 27 × 16 × 128 × 131072 × 2 B ≈ 29 GB at 131K — almost exactly your largest failing allocation (34,560 MiB after a doubling step). The fix is to branch on `kv_lora_rank` being present in config.json and use `layers × ctx × (kv_lora_rank + qk_rope_head_dim) × 2 bytes` for MLA models, ignoring num_key_value_heads entirely.

Our calculator does exactly this branch (MLA vs GQA read from config.json), which is why it reports ~19 GB for this model at 128K instead of "doesn't fit": https://modelfit-eight.vercel.app/guides/how-much-vram-for-llm/
